## Flagged, not guessed

- Paw Patrol's $0.4 billion of box office and home media on the Wikipedia row uses The Numbers citations accessed in 2024 and 2023. Deadline's $18 billion retail line was re-read. The older add-on was not.
- Call of Duty at exactly $40 billion is a rounded milestone, not a full product-line audit.
- Ne Zha 2 has three public grosses. The article reports all three.
- Wikipedia revision 1384822773 prints The Super Mario Galaxy Movie as `$1,013,2447,550`. Unused.
- Domestic franchise share of the US top ten was not recoded for 2025. The line stops at 2024.
- Original-medium, per-year, and the older Disney roll-up charts remain the 2019 file and are labeled that way.
- Streaming dollars are absent from this ledger. This reading does not claim that no free source anywhere reports them.
- FY2026 Disney segment revenue is not in the article. The 10-K was not filed.

## Holds

1. Revision clocks checked against the Wikipedia API. Film grosses re-read from Box Office Mojo and The Numbers. "Original" coding is stated in the article.
2. 11 October was the old scrape label. Revision 1379832186 is 12 October 2026, 04:29 UTC. 2026 box office is labeled partial.
3. DC's $46.1 billion is the 2026 list, not `franchise.csv`. Merchandise and other streams can overlap economically. Separate hero rows can overlap the MCU row.
4. Fox is the exhibit's "cash and stock" wording. FY2025 segments were read in the 10-K, and the prior-year column matches the old exhibit.
5. The 28-of-39 audit was re-run. The "43 additional US-origin properties" line did not match and is now 45. Hello Kitty, Mario and Gundam were re-parsed and had not moved since October.
6. Parent mapping is described as a rule, not a title opinion. Failure cases are examples, not a cause.
7. The streaming sentence is limited to this ledger.
