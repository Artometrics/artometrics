Artometrics FRANCHISE report — data provenance
=============================================

Primary franchise ledger (Charts 1–5, owner rollups)
----------------------------------------------------
File: franchise.csv (and raw/media_franchises.csv mirror)
Origin: TidyTuesday 2019-07-02 — Media Franchise Revenues
URL: https://github.com/rfordatascience/tidytuesday/tree/main/data/2019/2019-07-02
Upstream: Wikipedia “List of highest-grossing media franchises” (July 2019 scrape)
License: Community dataset; Wikipedia text is CC BY-SA 3.0 — revenue figures are editorial estimates, not audited financials.
SHA-256 (franchise.csv committed in repo): run `sha256sum articles/franchise/data/franchise.csv` before publish.

Disney acquisitions (Chart 8)
-------------------------------
File: disney_acquisitions.csv
Deal prices: SEC Form 8-K / 10-K acquisition notes and Wikipedia “List of mergers and acquisitions by Disney” with primary citations.
Franchise lifetime totals: summed from franchise.csv for named properties (mid-2019 Wikipedia estimates).

Disney segment revenue (Chart 9)
--------------------------------
File: disney_segment_revenue_fy2024.csv
The Walt Disney Company Form 10-K for fiscal year ended September 28, 2024 (filed November 2024).
Segment labels: Entertainment, Sports, Experiences (parks & products).

Theatrical franchise share (Chart 7)
------------------------------------
File: top10_franchise_share_by_year.csv
Method: For each calendar year, the domestic top 10 films by calendar gross (Box Office Mojo yearly chart pages).
Classification: “Franchise installment” = sequel, prequel, spin-off, or shared-universe entry of an existing IP (manual Artometrics coding).
Source index: data/box_office_mojo_yearly_urls.csv

Studio library comparison (Chart 6 owner bars)
----------------------------------------------
Derived in build_brief.R from franchise.csv owner field with Disney consolidation rules documented in data/owner_consolidation_rules.csv

Franchise failure casebook (prose)
----------------------------------
File: franchise_failures.csv — editorial case rows with press/10-K citations (not revenue ranks).
