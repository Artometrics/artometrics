alphabet_youtube_ads_revenue.csv — Alphabet Form 10-K revenue disaggregation, "YouTube ads" (USD millions).

nielsen_tv_share_july_2025.csv — Same methodology, single month cross-distributor comparison.
creator_economy_tam_goldman.csv — Goldman Sachs Research creator-economy TAM (Apr 2023); projection labeled.
goldman_youtube_shorts_ad_share.csv — Goldman Sachs estimates for Shorts ads as % of YouTube ads (Apr 2023).
platform_revenue_fy2024.csv — FY2024 10-K crosswalk (YouTube ads vs Netflix total revenue; not same cost structure).
ypp_revenue_share.csv — Google/YouTube published partner revenue-share terms.
