# YOUTUBE — 60s vertical (9:16)

Hook: YouTube made thirty-six billion dollars in ads last year—while one podcast tripled its views… then lost the co-host.

Beat one: Nielsen says YouTube is thirteen point four percent of U.S. TV time—bigger than Netflix on the TV set.

Beat two: Google pays creators fifty-five percent on long-form ads. YouTube claims one hundred billion dollars paid out in four years.

Payoff: H3's Frenemies era tripled views. Nine months later, the ceiling was set. Platform growth does not fix a borrowed audience.
